# DataForSEO method

> PLACEHOLDER TO FOLD. DataForSEO publishes an official skill; fold it in
> here (keeping this frontmatter's name) the way socialite folds vendor
> skills — verbatim body, our frontmatter. Until then, the essentials:

- `serp_search(keyword, location)` — live organic results. Use for "who
  ranks", "what does the page look like", "is our brand present".
- `keyword_ideas(seed_keywords, location)` — suggestions with volume. Use
  for "what should we target", "how big is this topic".
- Every call costs credits. Batch questions before calling; never loop over
  a keyword list calling one at a time when one call takes twenty seeds.
- Volumes are estimates; trends across terms are more trustworthy than any
  single number. Say so when reporting.
